About us

Our Story

Over 25 years of bridal couture

Founded in 1995, Sposabella is an international bridal brand that has created the finest wedding dresses for 25 years. We design and make wedding dresses, concert gowns, party dresses and bespoke suits.

Sposabella opened in 1995 and has grown into an international bridal house known for haute-couture wedding dresses. Every gown is designed and produced by Sposabella — "we enjoy creating new concepts and design for our clients."
